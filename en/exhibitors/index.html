Exhibit at Vivaaha 2027

Present your offer directly to couples and families – for two days at Atrium Oberhausen. On stage: model, comedy and dance shows.

Exhibition spaceapprox. 4 m height × 3 m (large stand)
Stage & techProjector, screen, PA – approx. 12 min stage time per exhibitor
PromotionOn social media and flyers

Important points

  • No food or drinks may be sold – only 1 dl cups and small portions may be handed out.
